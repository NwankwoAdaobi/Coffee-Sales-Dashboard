# Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

## Overview
Interactive Power BI dashboard analysing 149K transactions across
3 New York store locations over 6 months ($698.8K revenue).

## Business questions
- How is revenue trending month to month?
- Which products and categories drive sales?
- When are the busiest hours and days, and how do stores compare?

## Dashboard pages
1. Executive Summary
2. Product Performance
3. Store Revenue Report

![Executive Summary](images/executive-summary.png)

## Key findings
- Revenue grew 103.8% from January to June
- Coffee and tea generated 66.7% of total sales
- Sales peak at 10 AM; weekdays make up 72% of revenue
